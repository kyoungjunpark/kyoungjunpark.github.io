RESEARCH

My work builds multimodal generative models that perceive and reason over audio, radio frequency, and video signals, and makes them efficient enough to deploy under real compute, bandwidth, and energy budgets. Representative topics:

  • Token efficiency in audio LLMs (in submission)
  • Multimodal diffusion models with visual and physical signals (CVPR Findings '26, one paper in submission)
  • AI-generated video detection and reasoning-based MLLMs (ICLR '26)
  • Video streaming in LEO satellite networks (INFOCOM '26)
  • Resource-efficient mobile and adaptive video systems (CoNEXT '24, TMC '22, INFOCOM '19)
